Member B. Go row by row. The keywords as a list of lists would keep the order of the file, but checking whether a word is a keyword means comparing it with every element. The two parallel lists could be one dictionary from category to its set, which cannot get out of step, but has no positions. The nested dictionary could be a nested list, the statement’s own example, which we show on the next slide. A list of tuples is easy to print but finding one count means searching the whole list. d_votes could be two parallel lists. And the list of words could be a dictionary of counts, faster for long texts, but it loses the order of the words, which the statement asks for.
